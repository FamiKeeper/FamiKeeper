Getting started

FamiKeeper lets parents and guardians review a WhatsApp account they are authorized to manage and receive locations shared with consent.

  1. Agree on what to share. Explain the features to the account owner and get the permission required where you both live.
  2. Follow the steps in the app. Link an account through WhatsApp's Linked devices feature, or send a location sharing link.
  3. Allow time to connect. Keep a working internet connection while the account links and its first data loads.

Common questions

Why won't my WhatsApp account link?

Check that WhatsApp is up to date and that both devices have an internet connection. Follow the linking instructions shown in FamiKeeper. If a QR code or linking code has expired, request a new one in the app.

Check Linked devices in WhatsApp for existing sessions. If it still fails, send support the error text and the approximate time it happened. Do not send a QR code, linking code, or password.

Why are messages or activity missing?

The first sync can take time. Check the account's connection status in FamiKeeper and whether its session is still listed in WhatsApp's Linked devices. If FamiKeeper asks you to reconnect, follow the steps in the app.

Available history depends on what WhatsApp supplies to the linked session. Some messages, media, or activity may be delayed or unavailable. FamiKeeper cannot guarantee a complete record.

How does location sharing work?

The recipient opens your sharing link and chooses whether to share their location. Their browser must have location permission and an internet connection. A link alone does not give you access to someone's location.

If the location stops updating, ask the recipient to check their permission and keep the sharing page open. The browser, device settings, network, and link expiry can affect updates.

How do I stop sharing or disconnect an account?

To disconnect WhatsApp, open WhatsApp on the account owner's phone, go to Linked devices, select the session used by FamiKeeper, and log out. You can also use the account's stop control in FamiKeeper.

For a location session, use the stop control on the sharing page or in FamiKeeper. The person sharing can also revoke the site's location permission in their browser. Stopping future updates does not by itself erase stored history. See privacy and deletion requests.

Does FamiKeeper read my photo library?

No. FamiKeeper requests permission to add photos or videos only when you choose to save media. It does not request access to read your photo library. You can change its Photos permission in iOS Settings.

Is FamiKeeper free?

The current version is free. If a future version offers paid features, the price and any subscription terms will be shown before you purchase. See our purchase terms.

Privacy and deletion requests

You can contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with FamiKeeper. You can also report an account or location shared without the required permission.

Email dongdongh0408@163.com with the subject FamiKeeper Privacy Request. Describe your request and the feature involved. We will ask for the minimum information needed to find the data and verify your authority.

Contact us before uninstalling if possible. Uninstalling does not send a request to delete server data, and an installation credential can remain in the iOS Keychain. If you have already uninstalled, you can still contact us for help.

Do not send passwords, verification codes, linking QR codes, private chat histories, or another person's precise location. Our Privacy Policy explains retention and deletion.

Contact us
