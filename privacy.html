At a glance

Linked account content and shared locations are processed on our servers to provide the app's features. FamiKeeper does not sell personal information, show ads, or use third-party analytics SDKs. You can stop a connection and contact us about your data.

This policy explains how the FamiKeeper developer ("we", "us") processes personal information when you use the FamiKeeper iOS app and this support website. Contact us at dongdongh0408@163.com.

1. Information we process

  • Installation and service information. The app creates an installation identifier and a signing key. Credentials are stored in the iOS Keychain. Our servers use the installation identity to authenticate requests and connect them to your app data. The app can send its version, iOS version, and region. The service also receives your IP address and request details when you connect. We do not use the advertising identifier (IDFA).
  • Linked WhatsApp account data. When you authorize a connection through WhatsApp's Linked devices feature, our servers maintain the linked session and process available account and contact details, phone numbers, profile images, chats, messages, media, message edits and deletions, and activity such as online status. The app stores local copies of synchronized content so you can review it.
  • Shared locations. When a recipient opens a sharing link and grants permission, their location, accuracy, and timestamps are sent to our servers. We process the sharing link, session status, and available location history to show them in your app. The app can use Apple's map and address services to display locations and resolve coordinates to addresses.
  • Local settings and diagnostics. Account labels, app preferences, language settings, cached content, and local diagnostic logs are stored on your device. Local diagnostic logs are not automatically sent to an analytics service.
  • Saved media. FamiKeeper requests add-only Photos permission when you choose to save a photo or video. It does not read your photo library. Media you save to Photos is separate from the app's storage.
  • Support messages. If you contact us, we receive your email address, message, and any attachments you choose to send. Please remove unnecessary personal information from attachments.

2. How we use information

We use information to authenticate your installation, maintain authorized connections, synchronize and display account content, show shared locations, respond to support and privacy requests, prevent misuse, and keep the service working.

We do not sell personal information, use it for targeted advertising, or track you across other companies' apps or websites. The current app does not include third-party advertising or analytics SDKs.

Where applicable law requires a legal basis, we process information as needed to provide the service you request, with consent where required, to meet legal duties, or for legitimate interests such as service security and support, subject to your rights. A person's permission to link an account or share a location can be withdrawn.

3. Who receives information

Authorized users of a linked account can see the content made available through that connection. A location sharing session makes the shared location available to the person who created the request. Protect access to your device and sharing links.

Service providers process information as needed to host and operate our servers, storage, and support email. We may also disclose information when required by law or when necessary to address fraud, abuse, or threats to people's safety.

WhatsApp provides its linked-device service under its own terms and privacy policy. Apple processes information used by its operating system, Maps, and App Store services under its own policies. These services are not controlled by FamiKeeper.

Service providers may process information outside your country. Where required, applicable safeguards must be used for international transfers. Contact us for information about the handling of your data.

4. Retention and deletion

We retain linked account content and location records as needed to provide synchronization and the history available in the app. Support information is retained as needed to handle your request. Security or legal records may need to be kept for their applicable purpose or as required by law.

Disconnecting stops future access; it is not a request to erase all stored data. You can log out the linked session from WhatsApp's Linked devices settings and use FamiKeeper's stop controls. A location session can be stopped from its sharing page or the app. Previously stored content or history may remain.

To request deletion of server data associated with your installation or a linked account, email dongdongh0408@163.com. We will verify your authority, identify the relevant data, and handle the request within the period required by applicable law. If some records must be retained, we will explain the reason and scope.

Deleting the app removes its local app storage but does not notify us to delete server data. Keychain credentials may remain after uninstalling. Media saved to Photos and copies in device or cloud backups must be managed separately. Contact us before uninstalling when possible so we can help identify your installation.

5. Your choices and rights

  • Disconnect a WhatsApp session in WhatsApp's Linked devices settings to stop that session's future access.
  • Stop a location sharing session, or revoke the sharing site's location permission in the browser.
  • Change FamiKeeper's Photos permission in iOS Settings.
  • Contact us to request access, correction, deletion, or a copy of your personal information.

Depending on where you live, you may also have rights to restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, receive portable data, or complain to a data protection authority. Withdrawing consent does not affect processing that was lawful before withdrawal. We may need to verify your identity or authority before acting on a request.

You can contact us even if you do not use FamiKeeper but believe your information has been processed through it.

6. Children and consent

FamiKeeper is intended for parents and guardians aged 18 or older. It is not a service for children to register for independently. A linked account may contain a child's information. Parents and guardians must have the legal authority to manage that account and provide any notice and obtain any consent required by applicable law, including the account owner's consent where required.

Being a family member does not automatically authorize access. Do not link another adult's account without informed consent. If you believe a child's or another person's information was provided without the required authority or consent, contact us so we can investigate and address it.

7. Security

The app connects to our service over HTTPS and keeps installation secrets in the iOS Keychain. No system can guarantee complete security. Keep your device protected and do not share passwords, verification codes, or linking credentials.

Content made available through an authorized WhatsApp linked session is processed by our service and cached in the app. Do not assume it exists only on the original WhatsApp device.

8. This support website

This is a static website hosted on GitHub Pages. We do not add analytics scripts, advertising trackers, cookies, or browser storage to these pages. GitHub receives technical information when it serves the website, such as your IP address and request details, under the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Email links open your email application. Nothing is sent to support until you send the email. Your email provider processes the message under its own terms.

9. Changes to this policy

We will post changes on this page and update the effective date. If a change requires notice or consent under applicable law, we will provide that notice or seek that consent.
